who handles your data

FZGC di Filippo Zanini & C. S.n.c., Viale della Repubblica 159, 59100 Prato, vat no. 02596310975. for any question: ciao@xyzxyz.it.

what data

the first name, last name and email you enter in the form, plus the date and time you subscribed and confirmed, which we need to prove your consent.

what for

only to send you the xyz_xyz newsletter: exhibitions, openings and events at the space.

legal basis

your consent, which you give by ticking the box and confirming from the email you receive (article 6(1)(a) of eu regulation 2016/679, known as gdpr).

is it required?

no: subscribing is your choice. without the requested data, though, we can’t send you the newsletter.

who else sees it

the newsletter is sent through sender, a service of uab sender.lt (vilnius, lithuania), which stores the data on servers in the european union and processes it only on our behalf. we never sell or pass on your data.

opens and clicks

sender records whether you open the emails and click the links. we only use this to understand how much the newsletter is read.

how long

until you unsubscribe. after that, your address stays only on the list of people who don’t want to receive anything, so we don’t write to you by mistake.

your rights

you can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use or to get a copy. you can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before: there’s an unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email, or write to ciao@xyzxyz.it. if you think your data is mishandled, you can complain to the italian data protection authority, the garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

cookies

this page doesn’t use cookies of its own and doesn’t track your visit. the newsletter form is shown by sender: to load it, your browser connects to sender’s servers, which receive technical data such as your ip address and count how many times the form is seen. the fonts of the page are on our own site. like any website, the page reaches you through a hosting service, github pages, run by github, inc. in the united states, which receives the technical data needed to show it, such as your ip address, and keeps it for security purposes.
